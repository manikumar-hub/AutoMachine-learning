The problem

Machine learning is powerful, but getting started is hard.

Most people with useful data never get a model out of it. Here is what stops them, and what AutoML AI does about it.

Problem

ML needs code and years of practice

Solution

Upload a CSV and the whole pipeline runs for you, step by step, with no code.

Problem

Real data is messy

Solution

Automatic profiling, duplicate and missing-value handling, and warnings for ID-like, constant or mostly-empty columns.

Problem

It is hard to know which model is best

Solution

Several models are trained side by side and scored with the right metrics, so the best one is clear at a glance.

Problem

Results are full of jargon

Solution

AI Insights explains your metrics, your dataset, or any file you attach, in simple words.

Problem

You have data but no labels

Solution

The clustering lab finds natural groups in your data and describes what makes each group different.

Problem

Shared tools can mix up your data

Solution

Every user gets a private workspace, and sign-in is verified on the server for each request.

How it works

From raw file to usable model in four steps.

1

Upload

Drop in a CSV. You instantly see rows, columns, types, missing values and duplicates.

2

Choose

Pick what to predict. The app detects whether it is classification or regression and tells you why.

3

Train

Data is cleaned and encoded, then several models are trained and ranked on a held-out test set.

4

Understand and use

Read the AI explanation, enter new values to get predictions, and save the project to come back later.

Features

Everything you need, nothing to install.

Classification and regression

Logistic and linear models, decision trees, random forests and gradient boosting, compared with accuracy, precision, recall, F1, R² and error metrics.

Clustering lab

K-Means, Gaussian Mixture, Hierarchical, BIRCH and DBSCAN, with silhouette scores, a 2D map of your groups, and a CSV with a cluster for every row.

Cross-validation

Re-test your model on 5 different splits of your data to see whether its score is steady or just lucky.

AI Insights

Ask questions about your results, or attach a data file and get a plain-language summary of what is in it. Bring your own AI key.

Predict on new records

Type in values and get a prediction right away, or download the trained model for later use.

Private and saved

Sign in, keep your projects and datasets in your own account, and pick up where you left off.
